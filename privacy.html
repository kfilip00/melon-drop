Privacy Policy for Melon Drop

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Melon Drop ("the app", "the game") is published by Melon Drop ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app uses, who receives it and what choices you have.

Contact: yohanna.melondrop@gmail.com | Website: https://kfilip00.github.io/melon-drop/

1. The short version

  • The game works without an account. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or exact location.
  • Your game progress (coins, skins, best score, settings) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • The app shows ads. The ad companies we use (Google AdMob and Unity Ads) collect some device data to show and measure ads. Details are in section 3.
  • Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time (section 5).

2. Data that stays on your device

The app stores coins, unlocked skins, best score and settings in the device's local storage. This data is not uploaded. Deleting the app deletes it.

3. Data collected by advertising services

The app uses Google AdMob to show banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads. Unity Ads takes part through AdMob mediation, so it can also deliver ads. These services run inside the app and may collect:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID, or Apple IDFA when you allow tracking), and other device identifiers such as the app set ID.
  • IP address, from which an approximate location (country or region) can be estimated. We do not receive or use precise location (GPS).
  • Device and software information, such as model, operating system version, language, screen size, battery and network type.
  • Ad interactions, such as ads shown, taps and video views.
  • App and SDK diagnostics, such as performance, crashes and loading times.

They use this data to show ads (personalised ads only if you agree, where consent is required), measure ads, limit fraud and keep the services secure. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. We only see summary ad reports.

Rewarded ads are optional. You watch them only if you tap the button to get a reward.

4. What we do not collect

We do not run our own servers for the game, our own analytics, user accounts, social logins, chat, payments or crash reporting. We do not sell your personal data.

5. Your choices

  • Consent message. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states, the app shows a consent message the first time you open it. You can accept, decline or manage your choices.
  • Change your choice. Open Settings in the game and tap Privacy Options. The button appears only where a consent message applies.
  • iOS tracking. On iPhone and iPad, the app asks permission before using the IDFA to track you across other companies' apps. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Advertising ID. On Android, go to Settings > Privacy > Ads to reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation. On iOS, use Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising.
  • Non-personalised ads. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they are not based on your interests.

6. Legal basis and your rights

EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR / UK GDPR). We rely on your consent for personalised advertising and for storing or reading identifiers on your device. You can withdraw consent at any time (section 5). You have the right to access, correct, delete and port your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because we do not hold personal data about you, requests about advertising data are usually best sent to the ad companies in section 8. You can still contact us and we will help.

United States. Residents of some states (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas and others) have the right to know, delete, correct and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, including targeted advertising. Advertising identifiers shared with ad companies may count as "sharing" under these laws. You can opt out with the Privacy Options button in the game where it is shown, or through your device settings. We will not discriminate against you for using these rights.

Other regions. You may have similar rights under your local law. Contact us at yohanna.melondrop@gmail.com.

7. Children

Melon Drop is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets that age). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below that age. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us at yohanna.melondrop@gmail.com and we will work with the ad companies to remove the data. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

  • Unity Ads (through AdMob mediation): https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Apple (App Store, tracking settings): https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google Play: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The app may contain links to these pages or to app store pages. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of other sites.

9. Data transfers and security

Ad companies may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. They use safeguards such as standard contractual clauses where the law requires them. Data sent by the ad SDKs is encrypted in transit.

10. Retention

Local game data stays on your device until you delete the app or clear its data. The ad companies keep advertising data under their own retention rules, which are in their policies above.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we will say so in the app or on the store page.
