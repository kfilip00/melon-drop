Support

Need help with Melon Drop, found a bug or have an idea? Email us at yohanna.melondrop@gmail.com. Please include your device model and the app version (shown on the store page) so we can help faster.

How do I play?

Drag to aim and release to drop a fruit into the jar. When two matching fruits touch, they merge into the next, bigger fruit. Keep the pile below the top line: if it goes over, the run ends. The NEXT bubble shows which fruit comes after the current one.

Where is my progress saved?

Your coins, skins, themes, best score and settings are stored on your device only. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. If you delete the app or clear its data, your progress is deleted too, and it does not move to a new device.

Are the ads optional?

Banner and interstitial ads are part of the free game. Rewarded ads are always optional: you only watch one when you tap the button to double your coins or to continue a run once.

How do I change my ad consent choice?

Open Settings in the game and tap Privacy Options. You can accept, decline or manage your choices there. The button appears in regions where a consent message applies, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states.

How do I change tracking or ad personalisation?

  • iPhone and iPad: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising.
  • Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads, where you can reset or delete your advertising ID.

Something is not working

Try closing the game completely and opening it again, and make sure you have the latest version from the store. If ads do not load, check your internet connection: the game itself works offline, but ads and rewards need a connection. If the problem continues, email us and describe what happened.
